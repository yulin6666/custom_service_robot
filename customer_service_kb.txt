Enterprise Internal Knowledge Base - Employee Frequently Asked Questions

===================
I. Administration Management
===================

Q: How do I request office supplies?
A: Office supplies request process:
1. Log in to the corporate OA system and go to the "Administration Management" module
2. Click "Office Supplies Request", fill in the request form (item name, quantity, purpose)
3. Submit for approval by your department manager
4. After approval, the Administration Department will deliver to your workstation within 3 business days
Common office supplies (pens, notebooks, staplers, etc.) can be picked up directly at the Administration front desk without a request.

Q: How do I book a meeting room?
A: There are two ways to book a meeting room:
1. Online booking: Log in to the OA system "Meeting Room Booking" module, select time, number of attendees, and meeting room, then submit the booking request
2. Phone booking: Call the Administration Department at extension 8888, and an admin specialist will assist with the booking
Booking notes:
- Book at least 2 hours in advance
- Large meeting rooms (30+ people) require 1 day advance booking
- If you need a projector, whiteboard, or other equipment, please note it in the remarks
- To cancel a booking, notify at least 1 hour in advance; otherwise it will affect your personal credit score

[Evening Shuttle]

Q: How do I request courier service?
A: Courier service request process:
1. Prepare the items to be sent and the recipient's information
2. Log in to the OA system "Courier Request" module and fill in the shipping information
3. Bring the items to the Administration front desk; admin staff will handle the shipment
4. Courier fees are covered by the company (personal items excluded)
Personal packages can be delivered to the company. Use the following address: XX Building, XX Floor, Administration Front Desk, No. XX, XX Road, XX City. Phone: XXX-XXXX.

Q: What should I do if I lose my employee badge?
A: Lost badge process:
1. Immediately report to your department manager and the Administration Department
2. Log in to the OA system and fill in the "Badge Replacement Request"
3. Go to the Administration front desk for a photo and pay the processing fee of 50 RMB
4. A new badge can be collected within 3 business days
Temporary entry is allowed by registering with your ID card, but the replacement must be completed within 5 business days.

II. Human Resources
===================

Q: How do I apply for annual leave?
A: Annual leave application process:
1. Log in to the OA system "Leave Management" module
2. Select "Annual Leave" type, fill in the start/end dates and reason
3. Submit for approval by your direct manager
4. After approval, HR will deduct the corresponding leave days in the system
Annual leave rules:
- Employees with 1+ year of service receive 5 days of annual leave; 1 additional day per year, up to 15 days
- Annual leave must be applied for 3 days in advance; special cases may be applied retroactively
- Annual leave is valid for the current year and does not carry over to the next year
- Check remaining annual leave in the OA "My Leave" module

Q: When is salary paid?
A: Company salary is paid on the 15th of each month (postponed to the next business day if it falls on a holiday). Salary components include:
- Base salary
- Position allowance
- Performance bonus (based on previous month's evaluation)
- Perfect attendance bonus (no tardiness or early departure in the month)
- Overtime pay (calculated based on actual overtime hours)
Deductions include: social insurance, housing provident fund, personal income tax, etc.
Payslips can be downloaded in the OA system "Salary Inquiry" module. For questions, please contact the HR Department.

Q: What are the social insurance and housing provident fund contribution rates?
A: Social insurance and housing provident fund contribution rates:
[Social Insurance]
- Pension insurance: Individual 8%, Company 16%
- Medical insurance: Individual 2%, Company 9.5%
- Unemployment insurance: Individual 0.5%, Company 0.5%
- Work injury insurance: Individual 0%, Company 0.5%
- Maternity insurance: Individual 0%, Company 0.8%
[Housing Provident Fund]
- Individual 12%, Company 12%
The contribution base is the average monthly salary of the previous year, adjusted every July. To check your personal social insurance and provident fund account information, log in to the local social insurance/provident fund official website.

Q: How do I apply for an internal transfer?
A: Internal transfer application process:
1. Check the "Internal Recruitment" module in the OA system for available positions
2. After identifying the target position, communicate with your current department manager
3. With manager approval, fill in the "Internal Transfer Application Form"
4. Attend an interview with the target department
5. After passing, HR will handle the transfer procedures
Note: You must have worked in your current position for at least 1 year before applying for a transfer. The probation period after transfer is 1 month.

Q: How do I register for employee training?
A: Employee training registration:
1. Follow training announcements in the OA system "Training Center"
2. Select a training course of interest and click "Register"
3. Fill in the registration information and submit
4. You will receive an SMS/email reminder 1 day before the training
Training types include:
- New employee onboarding training (mandatory)
- Professional skills training (optional)
- Management capability training (for managers and above)
- External training (requires department manager approval; company covers the cost)

Q: What is the resignation process?
A: Employee resignation process:
1. Submit a written resignation to your direct manager 30 days in advance
2. Fill in the OA system "Resignation Application Form"
3. Complete work handover and fill in the "Work Handover Checklist"
4. Complete resignation procedures:
   - Return badge, office supplies, computer, and other company property
   - Settle outstanding loans, reimbursements, and other financial matters
   - IT Department deactivates account permissions
   - HR Department handles resignation certificate, social insurance transfer, etc.
5. Finance Department settles final salary (paid on the 15th of the following month)
Employees in probation must apply 3 days in advance.

III. IT Office
===================

Q: What should I do if I forget my OA system password?
A: Ways to reset your OA system password:
1. Self-service reset: On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and reset via verification code sent to your registered phone number
2. IT support: Call the IT service desk at extension 6666, provide your employee ID and identity verification, and a technician will assist with the reset
3. In-person: Bring your badge to the IT Department office
To ensure account security, passwords must contain uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters, be at least 8 characters long, and must be changed every 90 days.

Q: How do I request new software or system permissions?
A: Software and system permission request process:
1. Log in to the OA system "IT Services" module
2. Select "Software/Permission Request"
3. Fill in the request information: software name, purpose, usage period
4. Submit for approval by your department manager and the IT Department
5. After approval, the IT Department will complete the configuration within 2 business days
Common office software (Office, browsers, etc.) is pre-installed and does not require a request. Specialized software (design tools, development tools, etc.) requires authorization.

Q: What should I do if my computer has a problem?
A: Computer issue handling process:
1. For simple issues (e.g., software freezing), try restarting first
2. If unresolved, call the IT service desk: 6666
3. Describe the issue and its urgency
4. An IT engineer will provide remote assistance or come on-site
Issue response times:
- Critical issue (unable to work): Response within 30 minutes
- General issue: Response within 4 hours
- Non-urgent issue: Resolved within 1 business day
To replace hardware (keyboard, mouse, monitor, etc.), submit a "Hardware Replacement Request" in the OA system.

Q: How do I connect to the company VPN?

1. Download the VPN client: Log in to the OA system "IT Services" - "VPN Download"
2. Install and run the client
3. Enter your VPN account (employee ID) and password (OA password)

VPN usage rules:

- Prohibited for downloading large files or P2P downloads
- For connection issues, contact the IT service desk
- First-time users must complete identity verification at the IT Department

IV. Legal & Compliance
===================

Q: How do I apply for contract review?
A: Contract review application process:
1. Prepare the contract draft (Word format)
2. Log in to the OA system "Legal Services" - "Contract Review"
3. Upload the contract file and fill in contract information (contract type, amount, counterparty, etc.)
4. Submit for review by the Legal Department
5. The Legal Department will provide review feedback within 3 business days
Major contracts (amount exceeding 1 million RMB or involving significant rights) require 7 business days for review; plan your timeline accordingly.

Q: What are the main contents of the company's non-disclosure agreement?
A: The employee NDA mainly includes:
1. Scope of confidentiality:
   - Company trade secrets (client lists, pricing systems, market strategies, etc.)
   - Technical secrets (technical solutions, R&D data, source code, etc.)
   - Financial data (financial statements, cost accounting, investment plans, etc.)
2. Confidentiality period: During employment and 2 years after resignation
3. Confidentiality obligations:
   - Must not disclose to third parties
   - Must not use for personal or other organizations
   - Properly safeguard confidential documents and materials
4. Liability for breach: Violating the agreement incurs legal liability and compensation for losses
All employees must sign the NDA upon onboarding. Please comply strictly.

Q: How are intellectual property issues handled?
A: Intellectual property handling principles:
1. Work-related results belong to the company:
   - Inventions, software copyrights, trademark designs, etc. completed during employment belong to the company
   - The company has the right to apply for patents and copyright registration
2. Patent application process:
   - Submit a "Patent Application Form" to the Legal Department
   - The Legal Department conducts a preliminary review
   - Engage a patent agency to handle the application
3. Copyright registration:
   - Software, written works, etc. can apply for copyright registration
   - Submit the application and work materials to the Legal Department
If you discover any infringement, please report it to the Legal Department promptly.

Q: How can employees file complaints or report violations?
A: Complaint and reporting channels:
1. Internal complaints:
   - Report to your direct manager or the HR Department
   - Call the complaint hotline: 8888-1234 (anonymous allowed)
   - Send an email to: complaint@company.com
2. Serious violations (corruption, commercial bribery, etc.):
   - Call the group audit hotline: 400-XXX-XXXX
   - Send an email to: audit@group.com

   - Log in to the company intranet "Integrity Reporting Platform"
The company protects whistleblowers' rights and strictly prohibits retaliation. Verified reports may be rewarded.

V. Financial Reimbursement
===================

Q: How do I reimburse travel expenses?
A: Travel expense reimbursement process:
1. Submit a "Business Trip Application" in the OA system before the trip and get it approved
2. Keep all relevant receipts during the trip (flight tickets, train tickets, hotel invoices, meal invoices, etc.)
3. Submit reimbursement within 7 days after the trip:
   - Log in to the OA system "Expense Reimbursement" - "Travel Expense Reimbursement"
   - Upload scanned receipts
   - Fill in expense details (transportation, accommodation, meals, other)
4. Submit for approval by your department manager and the Finance Department
5. After approval, the amount will be transferred to your salary card within 10 business days
Travel expense standards:
- Transportation: Economy class/second class (Directors and above may choose business class/first class)
- Accommodation: 300 RMB/day (Manager level), 500 RMB/day (Director level), 800 RMB/day (VP level)
- Meals: 100 RMB/day
- Local transportation: Actual cost reimbursed

Q: How do I reimburse daily expenses?
A: Daily expense reimbursement process:
1. Confirm the expense type (office expenses, communication fees, transportation, entertainment, etc.)
2. Prepare invoices (must be official invoices with the company name)
3. Log in to the OA system "Expense Reimbursement" - select the corresponding expense type
4. Upload scanned invoices, fill in expense details and purpose
5. Submit for approval (amounts over 1,000 RMB require department manager approval; over 5,000 RMB requires Finance Director approval)
6. After approval, paid with salary on the 15th of the following month
Notes:
- Invoices must be submitted within 3 months of the invoice date
- Entertainment expenses must include a client sign-in sheet and meeting minutes
- Office supply purchases must include a procurement request form

Q: How do I verify invoice authenticity?
A: Invoice verification methods:
1. National Tax Administration VAT Invoice Verification Platform:
   - Website: https://inv-veri.chinatax.gov.cn
   - Enter invoice code, number, date, amount, and other information to verify
2. Local tax bureau official website or WeChat public account
3. Call the 12366 tax service hotline
It is recommended to verify invoice authenticity before submitting for reimbursement. Fake invoices will not be reimbursed and may involve legal liability.

Q: Can the company withhold personal income tax on my behalf?
A: Yes, the company withholds and remits personal income tax as required by law.
1. Salary income:
   - Automatically pre-withheld when salary is paid each month
   - Calculated using the cumulative withholding method
2. Annual tax reconciliation:
   - Annual reconciliation for the previous year is conducted from March to June each year
   - The company provides tax records and assistance services
   - Employees can also handle it themselves through the Personal Income Tax app
3. Special additional deductions:
   - Children's education, continuing education, serious illness medical expenses, housing loan interest, housing rent, elderly care
   - Please report in the Personal Income Tax app or submit relevant certificates to the HR Department
   - Update next year's deduction information before December each year
To check personal tax records, log in to the Personal Income Tax app or request from the Finance Department.

Q: How do I apply for petty cash?
A: Petty cash application process:
1. Confirm the purpose (e.g., procurement, business trip, event preparation, etc.)
2. Log in to the OA system "Financial Management" - "Petty Cash Application"
3. Fill in the requested amount, purpose, and estimated usage period
4. Submit for approval by your department manager and the Finance Department
5. After approval, collect cash or receive a transfer from the Finance Department
Petty cash usage rules:
- Single application not to exceed 5,000 RMB (special cases require Director approval)
- Reimburse and return remaining funds within 15 days of use
- Overdue reimbursement will be deducted from salary and affect credit records

VI. Procurement Management
===================

Q: How do I initiate a procurement request?
A: Procurement request process:
1. Confirm procurement needs (item name, specifications, quantity, purpose)
2. Log in to the OA system "Procurement Management" - "Procurement Request"
3. Fill in the procurement request form:
   - Item information (name, specifications, quantity, budget)
   - Requesting department, user, required delivery time
   - Upload technical specifications or sample images (if available)
4. Submit for approval:
   - Amount < 10,000 RMB: Department manager approval
   - Amount 10,000-100,000 RMB: Department director approval
   - Amount > 100,000 RMB: General manager approval
5. The Procurement Department will complete the purchase within 3 business days after approval
For urgent procurement, note it in the request form and the Procurement Department will prioritize it.

Q: How are suppliers selected for procurement?
A: Supplier selection principles:
1. Standard items:
   - The Procurement Department maintains a list of partner suppliers
   - Prefer suppliers from the list (reliable quality, competitive pricing)
2. Special items:
   - The requesting department may recommend suppliers
   - The Procurement Department will compare prices and review qualifications
   - Amounts over 50,000 RMB require quotes from at least 3 suppliers
3. New supplier onboarding:
   - Submit a "Supplier Onboarding Application"
   - Provide business license, qualification certificates, and other materials
   - The Procurement Department conducts evaluation and on-site inspection
   - After passing evaluation, the supplier is added to the supplier list
For quality complaints about suppliers, please contact the Procurement Department.

Q: How is goods acceptance handled after procurement delivery?
A: Procurement acceptance process:
1. When the supplier delivers, the recipient must verify:
   - Whether item name, specifications, and quantity match the purchase order
   - Whether the outer packaging is intact and undamaged
2. After signing, conduct unboxing inspection:
   - Check item appearance and functionality
   - Verify that accessories, manuals, etc. are complete
3. Acceptance result handling:
   - Accepted: Confirm in the OA system "Procurement Acceptance" module
   - Not accepted: Take photos as evidence, contact the Procurement Department for return/exchange
4. Asset registration:
   - Fixed assets (computers, furniture, etc.) must be registered with the Administration Department
   - Items received must be signed for
Acceptance period is 3 business days after receipt; overdue is considered accepted.

Q: How are procurement disputes handled?
A: Procurement dispute handling process:
1. Common dispute types:
   - Quality issues (items not as described, defects)
   - Delivery delays
   - Price disputes
   - Inadequate after-sales service
2. Handling process:
   - Contact the Procurement Department immediately with evidence (photos, chat records, etc.)
   - The Procurement Department coordinates with the supplier (return/exchange, compensation, etc.)
   - If negotiation fails, submit to the Legal Department for intervention
3. Preventive measures:
   - Sign procurement contracts specifying quality standards, delivery time, and liability for breach
   - Always inspect before payment
   - Keep procurement vouchers and communication records
For major disputes, please report to your department manager and the Procurement Department promptly.

Q: Can the company request invoices for procurement?
A: Yes, all company procurement must request official invoices.
1. Invoice requirements:
   - VAT special invoice (deductible) or VAT general invoice
   - Invoice header: XX Technology Co., Ltd.
   - Tax number: 91XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
   - Address and phone: No. XX, XX Road, XX City, Phone: XXX-XXXX
   - Bank and account: XX Bank XX Branch, Account: XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
2. Invoice purposes:
   - As payment basis
   - For financial accounting and tax deduction
   - Fixed assets require invoice registration
3. Handling without invoice:
   - Suppliers unable to provide invoices will not be paid
   - Special cases require General Manager approval
Always request invoices from suppliers during procurement; the Finance Department will assist in verifying invoice authenticity.
